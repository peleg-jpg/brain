# Follower Growth Over Views

The most contrarian principle in this framework: stop obsessing over views. The only metric that matters is **week-over-week follower growth percentage**. Views are vanity. Follower growth is the real signal. A creator with 3,000 followers growing at 6%/week will overtake a 50,000-follower account growing at 1%/week - and do it faster.

## Core Principles

### Views Are on Their Way Out

A fundamental misconception drives most creator anxiety: pulling up the page, seeing view counts down, and panicking. This is one of the biggest mistakes creators make. The core insight is compounding. A smaller account growing fast will overtake a larger account growing slow.

**The Math:**

- 50,000 followers growing at 1%/week = reaches 100K in 70 weeks (~1.5 years)
- 3,000 followers growing at 6%/week = reaches 100K in 60 weeks (~1.25 years)

The small account wins. The question to ask: would you rather have a 50,000 follower account growing at 1% week over week, or a 3,000 follower account growing at 6%? The answer should be obvious, but most creators chase the larger account because it looks better on paper.

### The Cigarette Analogy for Views

Compulsive view-checking works like a cigarette addiction. You pull up your page, see the view count, get a hit of dopamine or anxiety, and it changes nothing about your strategy. The compulsive checking of individual video views distracts from the metric that actually predicts your future: weekly follower growth rate.

The problem compounds: when you see a video with "low views," you panic and change strategy. But that video might have been your best follower-converter. The compulsion to check views leads to strategic whiplash.

### The Only Statistic That Matters

The only statistic that matters for you is week-over-week follower growth percentage.

Benchmarks for week-over-week follower growth:

- **10% week-over-week**: Phenomenal. One of the best creators on the planet. "Quit your job, go all in." Must average this over months, not just one or two viral videos. Extremely rare - clients averaging 100K followers/month territory.
- **5% week-over-week**: Really damn good. Exceptional growth. Content-market fit found. Scale.
- **3% week-over-week**: Solid. Growing meaningfully. Focus on optimizing what works.
- **1% week-over-week**: Average. Most creators who are "doing okay" sit here. Room for significant improvement.
- **Below 1%**: Below average. Time to reassess. Something fundamental is off - niche selection, counter-positioning, content quality, or follower quality.

Let week-over-week follower growth percentage be the dictator as you think about where your content is going.

**Critical caveat**: These must be averaged over months. One viral video can spike you to 10% for a week, but that does not count. The signal is the sustained rate after subtracting spikes.

### Views vs. Followers - The Disconnect

A real client example demonstrates this perfectly:

- **Video A**: Celebrity tie-in video - 400,000 views, 50 followers
- **Video B**: Super-detailed platform cold outreach tutorial - 100,000 views (25% of Video A), 4,000 followers

Twenty-five percent of the views, but 17 million percent more followers. That is the type of content to recreate.

The math is staggering. Video B had a follower-per-view rate of 4%, while Video A was at 0.0125%. Video B was 320x more effective at the metric that actually matters.

### Why Followers Per View Matters More Than Raw Views

The difference comes down to **uniqueness**. If they have heard it before, they will not follow. They already have that follower slot filled. People follow novelty.

Five categories of content drive followers per view:

1. **Unique value**: Most common reason people do not get followers - their advice is not unique enough. If you are repeating what a famous creator has already said, why would someone follow you? They already have that follower slot filled.
2. **Unique perspective**: Can you blow their mind on how they think about the world? Change their worldview? Not actionable tips, but a new way of thinking.
3. **Unique humor**: A word of warning - most creators are not actually funny and should not lean on this.
4. **Empathy**: Being able to relate to the viewer through shared lived experience.
5. **Aspirational content** (worst for followers): Doing a day in the life with a normal gym and normal food and a mildly decent body gets no follows. Millions of people online have mildly decent bodies.

### The Three Types of Viewers and Their Value

Not all followers are equal. Three viewer archetypes exist:

**Zero to Ones**: Have not started anything. 16-year-olds, total beginners. Need mindset content. You need to fill 2-3 football stadiums (300K+ quality views) because you can only charge $200-350 per person.

**One to Twos**: Already started. Looking to invest in themselves. Need tactics and skills. They are hungry for skills - they will follow and absorb the person who gives the most sauce. Charge $2-7K. Most educators target this group.

**Two to Threes**: Already successful, doing $1M+/year. Need extremely small incremental adjustments with exponential growth potential. Need strategy, not tactics. You do not care about views. You only care about inbound DMs. You need a dinner table, not a stadium.

The key question: would you rather have 1 million 12-year-olds watching your video, or 10 Fortune 500 CEOs watching your video?

If you do not know who you are targeting, you will attract the wrong followers and your follower quality tanks.

## The Doubling Test

When you increase posting volume, here is how to know if it is working:

### The Process

1. Open TikTok Studio
2. Click the arrow to get into deeper analytics
3. Toggle to "followers" at the top
4. Set to 28 days
5. Take your net follower count
6. **Subtract any spikes** from viral videos or tags - you want the flat baseline. For example, if there was a spike of 20,000 followers from being tagged, subtract that to get the real baseline.
7. Divide by 4 to get your weekly average
8. Monitor your 7-day metrics as you adjust volume

### The 80% Diminishing Returns Rule

When you double your posting volume, you want at least **1.6x-1.8x follower growth** (80% diminishing returns).

Example: If you average 1,000 followers/week posting once daily, and you move to twice daily, you should see at least 1,800 followers/week. If you are staying flat at 1,000 or dropping below, you have **follower fatigue** and your content quality is not good enough to support the volume yet.

This should be your gauge of whether to post more or compress back.

### Platform Difference - Critical

On TikTok, quantity is favored and you can get away with volume increases more easily. On Instagram, these same metrics matter 50 times more because it is a follower-first platform, and there is a higher chance of losing followers.

On Instagram, you need to be hyper-fixated on net follower growth when adjusting volume. Instagram punishes low-quality volume more severely because it is a follower-first platform - your content goes to followers first, and if they disengage, you spiral.

## The Accordion Method for Volume

The accordion method (credited to Caleb Ralston, Alex Hormozi's and Gary Vaynerchuk's creative director) is a cyclical compression-expansion framework:

### Phase 1: Compress (Learning Phase)

- **Post frequency**: 3-5x/week (beginners) or 1x/day
- **Focus**: Learning skill set. Rip the bandaid off. Get used to posting. Realize you will be completely fine. That person from college is probably going to unfollow you, but you have not talked to them in two years anyway.
- **Duration**: Until you consistently hit 1-5K views per video. May take 100 posts and iterations.
- **Learning resources**: Spend 1 hour daily learning. Caleb Ralston on YouTube, Sam Gaudet, Open Residency Podcast. Copywriting is a huge skill to prioritize.
- **Views per video**: 0-1,000

### Phase 2: Expand (Growth Phase)

- **Post frequency**: 2-3x/day
- **Focus**: Once hitting 1-5K views consistently. Volume leads to data.
- **The panic moment**: Views will get diluted into three videos. If you are usually getting 5,000 views a day, that breaks up into 2,000, 2,000, 2,000. But your channel is growing and that is what matters.
- **Testing split**: 70% proven formats, 30% wildcard experiments
- **Duration**: As long as you can. Grow to 10K-50K followers.
- **Warning**: If you are worried about the aesthetic of numbers on your profile because you are not getting 10,000 views per video, get over it.

### Phase 3: Compress Again (Innovation Phase)

- **Post frequency**: Back to 1x/day with higher quality
- **Focus**: You have plateaued (often around 50K followers). Time to reinvent.
- **Actions**: Find all your winning data. Hire an editor. Invest in a camera. Create a series. Invent new formats. Go higher production.
- **Duration**: Until you break through the plateau with a new winning format
- **Warning**: Do not re-pivot. Do not change niches. Compress.

### Phase 4: Expand Again (Scale Phase)

- **Post frequency**: 2-3x/day with the new innovative format
- **Team**: Hire an editor, maybe an ideator or strategist ($2-3K overseas)
- **Systems**: Build SOPs, max out trial reels (10x/day), cross-post everywhere
- **Goal**: Ride this to 100K-1M followers

Think of your time as an accordion. Press it to make fewer videos, expand it to make more.

### The Five Stages of Posting Frequency

The accordion expands into five explicit stages:

1. **Beginner**: 3-5x/week. Learning mode. One hour of learning daily.
2. **Skill acquired**: 2-3x/day. Put it on trials, move the winner over. Not on your main page.
3. **Signature formats found**: Back to 1x/day. Channel the volume data into proven signature formats and topics.
4. **Scaling**: 50K+ followers. Build systems, get an editor, learn SOPs. 2x/day with carousel in morning, signature format at night. Max trial reels.
5. **Long form**: Ready for YouTube/podcast as the next frontier.

## The 70/20/10 Split

While tracking growth, maintain this content ratio:

- **70%**: Proven formats and topics you know will perform
- **20%**: Tests of improvement on existing winning content
- **10%**: Completely off-the-wall experiments that make zero sense

You have to be so okay with videos not performing because you will not improve without testing. Many creators find their signature format through the 10% wildcard - a format they never would have tried if they stayed only with safe content.

## Algorithm Momentum and Follower Quality

### What Algorithm Momentum Is

When a video goes viral, it creates momentum for your next posts.

A real example: a client posted a viral carousel (300,000 views in 24 hours, 98% non-followers). The next video posted 12 hours later - which normally underperformed - became a 3x outlier.

This is the concept of algorithm momentum. After watching a funny or compelling piece of content from someone, you scroll and see that same person again. You have never seen them before. The algorithm is serving them to you because of the momentum from the first video.

**How to use it**: After a viral top-of-funnel video, immediately follow with educational or even sales content. The viral video's viewers are now being served your follow-up content. You can take a normally 1,000-view bottom-of-funnel video to 10-15,000 views by riding the momentum wave.

### What Happens When You Stop Posting

The flip side of momentum is devastating. Taking a month off from posting can cause baseline view averages to drop from 50,000 down to about 7,000. The reason is not the algorithm punishing you. It is your followers finding replacements. When you come back, you have to rework your way back into their rotation.

This is why maintaining a healthy relationship with content is the #1 skill:

Hooks that hold attention, storytelling that drives watch time, authentic personality, understanding data, editing, choosing the right ideas, building funnels, great communication, memorable brand, having the perfect counter position - there is only one correct answer and it is having a healthy relationship with content to post every single day. This is the combination of joy plus obsession.

The principle here draws from Roger Federer's autobiography "Mastering the Long Game of Tennis": Federer practiced the self-discipline of balance. When he was not feeling it, he would go to the gym and just talk to his trainer. When he lost, he would cry, eat with family. When he wanted to train, he would force himself to take vacation. The most important thing you can do is keep a healthy relationship with content. Think in decades, not days.

### The Verified Checkmark Data

Data across consulting and agency clients shows:

- **With verification**: ~3% profile visit to follower conversion rate
- **Without verification**: ~1-1.5% conversion rate
- **Over 12 months with 100,000 profile visits**:
  - Without checkmark: ~12,000 followers
  - With checkmark: ~43,000 followers
- **Plus compounding**: Those extra followers generate more views, which generate more profile visits, which generate more followers. The real gap is even larger than 4x.

That small monthly checkmark fee could theoretically grow your account close to 4x faster.

### Follower Quality Warning

Not all followers are created equal. Paid followers destroy your account because they do not engage, which tells the algorithm your content is bad, which gets you stuck in low-view jail. The worst-enemy blueprint looks like this: start with paid ads. Follower quality will be absolutely terrible. The account reaches 10,000 followers but gets a hundred views per video because those followers do not actually care. When a video is made, it gets served to followers first, they do not watch, and the algorithm marks it as a terrible video.

### Targeting Wrong Audiences

One wrong hook can attract the wrong audience permanently. A creator who opened a video with 45 seconds about a celebrity suddenly gained thousands of music followers they did not want.

The fix: be dialed to what your ideal customer profile (ICP) wants. Clearly speak to them in the first three to five seconds. If you are targeting hotel owners, do not just talk about travel destinations - say "if I was a hotel owner in this location, here is how I would capitalize on social media."

### The Concept of Follower Slots

People have a limited number of creator "slots" in their consumption rotation - maybe 5-10 favorites. When you stop posting, other creators fill your slot. When you post content that appeals to the wrong audience, you fill a slot in someone who will never engage with your real content.

One word in a hook targets a whole different demographic. Even the emotional undertone of your hook - the way you say it, the descriptors, the dream outcomes, the pain points - will get a whole different audience.

## The Creative Thought Life and Detachment

### Five Ways Creators Self-Sabotage Their Growth

Five mental blocks that cause creators to stall even when they have momentum:

1. **Thinking people are tired of old ideas**: David Ogilvy said "We do not market to a standing army, we make content to a marching crowd." About 15% of current followers will see both posts. Reframe ideas, clean them up, keep hitting them every two to four weeks.

2. **Thinking new ideas are not as powerful**: You do not know what your audience is going to like. You literally do not know.

3. **Format confusion**: "Do I keep doing the 10-minute rants? Do I need green screen? Skits?" This leads to paralysis by analysis. The fix: stick to 70/30. 70% what you know works, 30% test. Worst case scenario, just keep ripping your same format.

4. **Letting new high-status followers change your content**: When a blue check mark follows and you start getting in your head wondering if your content is good enough for them.

5. **Perfectionism**: For 99% of creators, you need to make content at volume to get data, not optimize for quality and perfectionism prematurely.

### The Detachment Principle

Great unscripted creators (yappers) share three traits:

1. **Reps**: Takes about 100 unscripted videos to get your personality across
2. **Communication tactics in their DNA**: From studying communicators and practicing
3. **Deep security in who they are**: An unscripted video is vulnerable. You have no idea if the world is going to like it. Great unscripted creators can detach themselves from the metrics, which keeps them in the game, keeps the volume high, and lets them improve.

## Cross-Posting and Volume Economics

### The 1:0.8 Return Ratio

Every time you cross-post or increase volume, there is approximately a 1:0.8 ratio of posts to returns:

- Posting 5x = making $5
- Posting 10x = making $9 (not $10, but close)

There is no decline, so you should take the time. If you are serious about building a personal brand and are not cross-posting to LinkedIn, X, and Threads, you are missing out. Hire someone to handle it, create an SOP. But focus on one platform first.

### Platform Selection

**YouTube**: 87% purchasing credibility metric (vs 56% IG, 49% TikTok). The more time people spend with you, the more they trust you. But terrible for discovery - you cannot easily reach new people on YouTube.

**Instagram/TikTok**: Discovery platforms. Infinitely better to reach new human beings through these platforms. Better for DMs and CTAs. Short form is raw skill set development.

**The order**: Start with short form (IG/TikTok) to build following, then funnel to long form (YouTube). If you start with long form, getting people over to it is very difficult.

## Tactical Playbook

### Weekly Growth Tracking Process

1. **Every Sunday**: Open TikTok Studio (or IG analytics). Pull 28-day follower count.
2. **Subtract spikes**: Any viral videos or tags that caused unnatural jumps. You want the baseline.
3. **Divide by 4**: Get your weekly average net follower growth.
4. **Calculate your growth %**: (Weekly net followers / Total followers at start of week) x 100
5. **Compare to benchmarks**: 10% = phenomenal, 5% = great, 3% = solid, 1% = average, <1% = problem
6. **Identify best converters**: Which videos got the most followers per view? Not total views. Use Edits app for detailed metrics.
7. **Plan next week**: Double down on high-converter formats and topics, not high-view formats.

### When to Increase Volume

1. Calculate your current weekly average (4-week baseline, spikes removed)
2. Start posting more (e.g., 1x/day to 2x/day)
3. After 2 weeks, recalculate
4. If new rate is >= 1.8x the old rate (for 2x the posting), keep going
5. If new rate is flat or declining, compress back to quality mode
6. On Instagram: watch net followers like a hawk. Any net loss = compress immediately.

### Follower Quality Audit

1. **Check who is following you**: Are they in your target demographic?
2. **Check engagement rate**: Are new followers actually watching your next videos?
3. **Audit your hooks**: Does the first 3-5 seconds clearly signal who this video is for?
4. **Kill broad hooks**: If a hook could appeal to anyone, it will attract non-ideal followers
5. **Never buy followers**: The algorithm reads their disengagement as "your content is bad"

### The Roger Federer Protocol for Longevity

1. Do not change niches. Stay in the family tree. If you are in fitness, you can go into nutrition, but you cannot randomly jump to space engineering.
2. When burned out, do not quit. Compress. Post less, higher quality.
3. Force balance: when you want to train, take a break. When you feel terrible, post anyway.
4. The best work is done in decades, not days.
5. Think about what it looks like to make not quitting part of your identity.

### Competition Is Not Real

From 100 people who start:

- 80 never really begin
- 10 quit too early
- 5 chase shiny objects
- 2 get unlucky
- 3 are your actual competition

The pattern in any niche: among any group of 15 apparent competitors, multiple will quit this year, others will change business models. The ones who keep going simply inherit the space.

## Key Principles (Condensed)

- Week-over-week follower growth percentage is the only metric that acts as a reliable compass.
- A 3,000-follower account at 6%/week beats a 50,000-follower account at 1%/week.
- Content with fewer views but a high follower-per-view rate is more valuable than high-view content with poor conversion.
- Volume and data matter above all. Most people just spray and pray. You have to analyze your data.
- Posting consistency is the single most important creator skill. Great hooks and storytelling mean nothing if you stop posting.
- Do not think in days. Think in decades.
