# How to Create Bottom-of-Funnel Content That Still Gets Views

## Core Insight

Most viral content produces no revenue because it has no connection to a product or service. The bottom of the funnel - content designed to drive purchases or inquiries - is typically the weakest performing part of a creator's output because it reads like an advertisement. The solution is not to stop creating bottom-of-funnel content but to make it indistinguishable from entertainment or education while still embedding the product naturally.

Three principles govern effective bottom-of-funnel content. First, the product must be embedded inside either an educational framework or a compelling story, never presented as the focus of the video. Second, basic copywriting principles must be applied throughout: lead with tangible benefits rather than features, and address the viewer's pain point directly. Third, the content must feel entirely organic - if it registers as a paid promotion or sponsored post, it immediately loses the majority of its reach. Bottom-of-funnel content should be produced roughly once or twice per week, not as the primary posting strategy.

## Key Tactics

- Embed the product inside an entertaining story or educational breakdown so the product appears as a natural solution discovered within the narrative, not as the point of the video.
- Replace feature descriptions with tangible outcome statements - not "the app has a timer feature" but "users reported lasting 40 minutes instead of 30 seconds."
- Anchor pain points early in the content so the product appears as a resolution the viewer arrives at themselves, rather than something being pushed on them.
- Include social proof organically within the narrative (the product went viral, a famous operator uses this method) rather than as a formal testimonial block.
- Keep bottom-of-funnel posts to roughly 10-20% of total output; flooding the feed with sales content erodes follower quality and engagement.
- Use a full content funnel structure: top-of-funnel for reach, middle-of-funnel for education, and bottom-of-funnel reserved for intentional conversion moments.

## Why It Works

Distribution is the bottleneck for any sales message. A piece of content that reads like an advertisement receives a fraction of the algorithmic reach that entertainment or education receives. By hiding the commercial intent inside a high-quality story or instructional video, the content competes for views against non-commercial posts and reaches a much larger pool of potential buyers. The total addressable market expands significantly, and the commercial message lands inside a context where the viewer has already decided to pay attention.
