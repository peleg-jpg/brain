# Breaking Through View Plateaus by Mining Reddit for Polarizing Topic Ideas

## Core Insight

The single most effective way to escape a stagnant view count is not to optimize production quality or posting frequency - it is to select a better topic. Topic selection is the highest-leverage variable in content performance, and Reddit is one of the most reliable sources for identifying ideas that already carry natural tension and audience investment.

The method works because Reddit's top posts within a subreddit represent subjects that real people in a niche care about enough to argue over publicly. By sorting any relevant subreddit by "Top - Month" (not Hot, New, or Controversial), a creator surfaces content that generated sustained engagement over 30 days - a proxy for lasting interest rather than momentary virality. The extracted argument structure then becomes the architecture for a video that positions the creator as a thoughtful, balanced analyst before ultimately taking a side - a move that drives comments, shares, and return viewership.

## Key Tactics

- Go to the relevant subreddit and sort by "Top - This Month" to find the highest-signal content without recency bias.
- Copy the entire thread including replies into a language model and prompt it to identify the two primary opposing stances people hold.
- Extract the exact vocabulary and phrasing both sides use - match that vernacular in your video script to signal in-group fluency.
- Structure the video to present both sides fairly through the middle portion, then commit to one position near the end to generate strong comment responses.
- One well-executed topic-mined video is sufficient to produce a meaningful outlier in view performance.

## Why It Works

Audiences engage most with content that mirrors their existing internal debates. When a video addresses a tension they already carry - using the same words they use to think about it - it creates immediate resonance that generic advice content cannot manufacture. The Reddit mining method essentially pre-validates topic demand before any production investment, turning content ideation from guesswork into market research.
