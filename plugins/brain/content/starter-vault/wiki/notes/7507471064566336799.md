# Finding Your Unclaimed Slice: How Counter Positioning Breaks You Into a Crowded Niche

## Core Insight

Counter positioning is the strategic practice of identifying underserved audience segments within an established niche and deliberately claiming that territory before others do. It is not enemy marketing - which involves attacking competitors - but rather a structural analysis of where audience attention already flows and where gaps exist. The mental model is a pie representing all the views in a niche: dozens of creators are competing for overlapping slices, while specific pockets of potential viewers remain underserved. The winning move is to find the slice that fits your authentic story and where competition is thin, not simply to chase whatever looks unoccupied.

The key discipline is relevance to self. Counter positioning only works when the gap you identify actually maps to who you are and what you can credibly create. Chasing an underserved demographic that does not match your own experience produces content that feels forced and fails to convert.

## Key Tactics

- Audit the top 15-20 accounts in your niche and catalogue the dominant themes, formats, and audience types they target - the gap is what you do not see represented.
- Counter position on TAM (total addressable market): if everyone targets the broadest possible audience, go hyper-specific; if everyone is hyper-niche, a slightly broader framing can stand out.
- Counter position on format: identify the 2-3 most common video formats in your niche and consider the structural opposite - a niche dominated by reaction green screens might have room for a direct-to-camera educational format, and vice versa.
- Counter position on brand association: identify the emotional stereotype your niche triggers in viewers and build a brand that activates a different, adjacent feeling - fitness content dominated by muscle-flexing could be countered with a gentlemen's styling angle for athletic builds.
- Use the high school cafeteria mental model: every niche contains the same human archetypes (the jocks, the nerds, the goths, etc.) - map those archetypes to your niche and pick the table that is nearly empty.

## Why It Works

Niches are not monolithic audiences - they are collections of sub-identities with distinct needs and vocabularies. When every major creator optimizes for the same broad demographic, the algorithm rewards the differentiated signal because it satisfies latent demand that existing content leaves unmet. Counter positioning works because it reduces competition at the point of discovery while still drawing from an established pool of interested viewers. Specificity earns loyalty faster than breadth because viewers feel the content was made specifically for them.
