# The Reddit Debate Method: A Three-Step System for Identifying High-Potential Video Topics

## Core Insight

Breaking out of low-view plateaus is primarily a topic selection problem, not a delivery or production problem. Creators who are stuck at a particular view ceiling are typically selecting topics that feel intuitive to them but do not carry the pre-existing audience tension required to generate organic spread. The principle taught is that the highest-performing video topics are not invented from scratch - they are discovered by locating existing debates that a real audience is already emotionally invested in, then entering those debates as a neutral synthesizer who eventually takes a side.

Real community debates carry three properties that invented topics typically lack: genuine audience investment (people care enough to argue), natural vocabulary (the words the audience uses to discuss the topic are already known), and built-in tension (two or more competing positions that create a narrative structure for the video). By entering an existing debate rather than manufacturing a new one, the creator inherits all three of these properties without having to create them.

## Key Tactics

- Identify the most active subreddits in the target niche and sort by "top of the month" - this surfaces the conversations generating the most sustained engagement within the community.
- Copy the full thread including replies into an AI tool and instruct it to identify the two primary competing positions and to extract the exact vocabulary and sentiment each side uses.
- Mirror the audience's own language in the video - using the same terms, phrases, and emotional register as the community signals authentic membership rather than outsider observation.
- Structure the video to present both sides neutrally through the middle section, creating a sense of fairness that holds viewers from both camps, then take a clear position at the end.
- Expect the first well-executed video using this method to significantly outperform average view counts - the combination of real tension, native vocabulary, and side-taking creates a high share-worthiness profile.

## Why It Works

Content that enters an existing debate gives the algorithm a pre-formed audience to distribute to - people who have already demonstrated interest in the topic. The neutral-then-opinionated structure maximizes retention across viewer segments by holding both sides until the end while the opinion close generates comments and shares from viewers who agree or disagree. This comment activity signals high-quality engagement to the distribution algorithm.
